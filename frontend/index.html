06. Analysis Logic & Safety References

How alerts are generated

What the analytics does

It combines live hazard states, manufacturer Rs/R0 gas reference estimates, official concentration thresholds as comparison references, rover-specific collision/stability rules, stale-link checks, ADC saturation checks and short-term gas-response trends.

Gas model: MQ-4, MQ-7, MQ-135 and MQ-3 use manufacturer-published Rs/R0 sensitivity relationships with the rover’s early clean-air baseline to provide reference concentration estimates. Raw ADC remains visible for traceability.

Official references used

  • DGMS mine ventilation rules: inflammable gas ≤1.25% at any place; return-air reference 0.75%; wet-bulb maximum 33.5 °C.
  • DGMS methane monitoring notification: alarm at 1% methane and power cut at 1.25%.
  • DGMS technical guidance: carbon monoxide maximum allowable concentration 50 ppm.
  • NIOSH: CO 35 ppm 8-hour REL, 200 ppm ceiling and 1,200 ppm IDLH.
  • NIOSH/OSHA noise references are shown only as future calibrated-dBA limits; the current sound module is binary.
