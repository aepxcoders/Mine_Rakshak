MineRakshak AI Analytics Threshold Basis
========================================

This test build uses an explainable risk engine, not a trained machine-learning model.

Regulatory / official reference values implemented:

1. Methane / inflammable gas (DGMS mine ventilation / methane monitoring references)
   - 0.75% (7,500 ppm): return-air reference limit in coal mine ventilation rules.
   - 1.0% (10,000 ppm): alarm reference used in DGMS methane drilling notification.
   - 1.25% (12,500 ppm): maximum inflammable gas at any place; 1.25% power cut trigger reference.
   - 5% (50,000 ppm): methane lower explosive limit reference.
   IMPORTANT: MineRakshak activates these only when calibrated ch4_ppm is supplied. Raw MQ-4 ADC is not ppm.

2. Carbon monoxide
   - 35 ppm: NIOSH 8-hour recommended exposure limit.
   - 50 ppm: DGMS maximum allowable concentration reference; also OSHA 8-hour PEL.
   - 200 ppm: NIOSH ceiling.
   - 1,200 ppm: NIOSH IDLH.
   IMPORTANT: MineRakshak activates these only when calibrated co_ppm is supplied. Raw MQ-7 ADC is not ppm.

3. Underground heat (DGMS ventilation reference)
   - Wet bulb >30.5 C: ventilation with air speed at least 1 m/s required.
   - Wet bulb >33.5 C: exceeds working-place wet-bulb limit.
   IMPORTANT: BMP280 supplies dry-bulb temperature, not wet-bulb temperature, so the current test hardware cannot claim heat-limit compliance.

4. Noise reference
   - NIOSH: 85 dBA 8-hour recommended exposure level.
   - OSHA: 90 dBA 8-hour PEL; 140 dB peak impulse reference.
   IMPORTANT: Current sound module is digital DETECTED/CLEAR only and cannot be compared with dBA limits.

Prototype operational rules (NOT statutory limits):
   - Obstacle warning <=25 cm; danger <=10 cm.
   - Rover tilt warning >=45 degrees; danger >=70 degrees.
   - Wi-Fi warning <=-75 dBm; danger <=-85 dBm.

Official sources researched for this build:
   - Directorate General of Mines Safety (DGMS), mine ventilation regulations / Gazette material.
   - DGMS methane monitoring/drilling notification: alarm at 1% methane, power cut at 1.25%.
   - DGMS technical guidance: carbon monoxide maximum allowable concentration 50 ppm.
   - NIOSH Pocket Guide / IDLH documentation for carbon monoxide.
   - NIOSH and OSHA occupational noise guidance.
